## Provenance rules

1. Derived datasets are labelled as project-derived and are not independent sources.
2. Hazard archives are scenario inputs, not probabilistic samples unless stated otherwise.
3. Facility registries differ in scope, level, year and inclusion rules from OSM amenity filtering.
4. WorldPop 2020 population is held constant across scenario dates.
5. OSM extracts are volunteer-created and spatially heterogeneous.
6. External accessibility products use different destinations and are not service-access ground truth.
