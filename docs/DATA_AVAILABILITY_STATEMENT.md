# Data availability

Derived tables (this deposit) are released under CC BY 4.0.  Upstream inputs keep their own
terms and are **not** redistributed here:

* Planet arterial road-surface predictions: HeiGIT/HDX product - see the source dataset's
  licence (the companion data paper) before redistributing any Planet-derived attributes.
* HOT/OSM road and facility exports: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
* WorldPop 2020 1 km population: CC BY 4.0 (WorldPop).
* GHS-SMOD 2020: European Commission JRC, reuse permitted with attribution.
* Hazard footprints: UNOSAT / OCHA / WFP via HDX, per-dataset terms.
* Independent healthcare travel-time benchmark: Malaria Atlas Project (Weiss et al. 2020);
  cite the original publication when reusing the comparison values.
* Weiss & Nelson 2015 city-access product: cite the original publication when reusing
  the comparison values.
* CPAS/Data for Children 100-m motorised health travel-time rasters (ETH/KEN/MOZ): source
  URLs, sizes and SHA256 in `data/external/accessibility_products/cpas/README.md`.
* SDG 9.1.1 Rural Access Index, 2023 Release: CIESIN/NASA SEDAC, CC BY 4.0,
  doi:10.7910/DVN/FFQ1WT; national/subnational CSVs are recorded with SHA256 in
  `data/external/accessibility_products/rai_sdg911/README.md`.

Analysis code: MIT licence (see the repository).
